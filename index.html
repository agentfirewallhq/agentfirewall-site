04  Process

How an engagement runs.

  1. You show us the setup

    A short walkthrough: which agents you run, what they can touch, and where money or promises are involved.

  2. We test the scenarios we agreed on

    Only the cases we defined together, run the way an ordinary user could. No real customer data.

  3. You get a ranked report

    Every weakness, what it could cost, and the fix, written so a founder can act on it, not just an engineer.

  4. We close the gaps

    Guardrails that block what we found, re-tested to prove each one is closed, with monitoring as your agent evolves.

05  Why now

What your agent says, your business owns.

Courts already agree. In 2024 Air Canada was ordered to honour a refund policy its support chatbot made up. Arguing that the bot was responsible for its own words didn't work.

Buyers are asking. Enterprise customers now send security questionnaires about your AI before they sign. An audit gives you the answers.

Regulation is arriving. New AI rules require governance and audit trails. Getting ready now costs far less than scrambling later.
